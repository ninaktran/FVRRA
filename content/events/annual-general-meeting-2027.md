The Fingal Valley Residents & Ratepayers Association invites all members and interested residents to our **Annual General Meeting 2026**.

## Agenda

- Welcome and apologies
- Minutes of the 2025 AGM
- President's report
- Treasurer's report and financial statements
- Election of committee members
- General business
- Questions from the floor

All financial members are eligible to vote. Membership must be current before the meeting to exercise voting rights.

Light refreshments will be provided.

**RSVP appreciated** — [contact us](/contact/) if you plan to attend so we can arrange seating.
