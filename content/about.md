## Who We Are

The **Fingal Valley Residents & Ratepayers Association Inc.** (FVRRA) is an incorporated community organisation representing the interests of residents and ratepayers in and around the Fingal Valley, Tasmania.

We are an independent, volunteer-run association committed to:

- **Advocating** for local residents on planning, infrastructure, and services
- **Connecting** community members through events and communication
- **Informing** residents about decisions that affect our valley
- **Preserving** the unique character and environment of Fingal Valley

## Become a Member

Membership is open to all residents and ratepayers in the Fingal Valley area. As a member you'll receive regular updates, have voting rights at general meetings, and be part of a strong community voice.
